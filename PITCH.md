# BuddyAI: 2-minute demo script

**0:00 Hook (15 s).** "Most healthcare workers infected with Ebola get it while taking PPE *off*. CDC says a trained observer should watch every doffing. In a busy isolation ward in Kenya there often isn't one. BuddyAI is that observer: a tablet running Gemma 4."

**0:15 Live Coach (45 s).** Log in as `worker@buddy.ai` and tap **Start doffing**.
- Show the CDC step and its cited source. The camera checks a frame every 6 s. *Buddy is checking…* appears, then the feedback is read aloud.
- Switch to **SW**: the same feedback is shown in Kiswahili.
- Try to skip a step. It's blocked, so log an override with a reason ("observer confirmed visually").

**1:00 Summary (20 s).** Finish the session. The risk score is rule-based and explainable (overrides, failed checks, flags). Gemma writes the coaching note in both languages. No frames were stored.

**1:20 Supervisor (25 s).** Log in as `supervisor@buddy.ai`. Show the 14-day activity, unit readiness, the violation feed, who's due for recertification and low PPE stock with QR labels.

**1:45 Open + Gemma 4 (15 s).** "Gemma 4 is Apache 2.0. The same code runs on the Gemini API or fully offline with Ollama, and next on a Raspberry Pi in a clinic with no internet. MERN, Apache 2.0, contributions welcome."

> Training and decision-support only. Not a medical device.
